# Privacy policy: Readwise for Even Realities G2

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Readwise for Even Realities G2 ("the app") is an independent app by Anton
Morrison. It is not made by or affiliated with Readwise, Anthropic or Even
Realities. This policy explains what the app stores, where it sends it, and why.

## The short version

- The app has **no server of its own**. It talks only to Readwise
  (`readwise.io`) and, if you add a Claude key, to Anthropic
  (`api.anthropic.com`).
- Your Readwise token and Claude key are **stored on your phone** and sent only
  to the service they belong to.
- There are **no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no accounts** with the
  developer.

## What the app stores on your device

Stored in the Even Realities app's storage on your phone:

- **Your Readwise access token**, which you enter in Settings.
- **Your Claude API key and chosen model**, if you add them in Settings.
- **A copy of your library**: titles, authors, publications, reading times and
  the publisher's note for documents in your Reader Inbox and Later, refreshed at
  most every 15 minutes or when you ask.
- **Summaries** the app has written for you, so they are not paid for twice.
- **Reading positions**: how far through each article you are.

None of this leaves your phone except as described below. Removing the Claude
key in Settings deletes it. Uninstalling the app removes the rest.

## What is sent, and to whom

**To Readwise (`readwise.io`)**, using your access token:
- requests to list your documents and fetch an article's text;
- a request to move an article to Archive, when you archive it.

**To Anthropic (`api.anthropic.com`)**, only if you have added a Claude API key,
and only when you ask:
- when you choose **Summarise**: the article's title, author, and up to the
  first 6,000 characters of its text;
- when you open the **Feed**: the titles and publication names of up to 120
  Feed items, so they can be sorted into themes.

Anthropic processes these requests under its own terms and privacy policy, on
your Anthropic account. The app does not use your data to train any model.

Nothing is sent to the developer. The app has no backend.

## Permissions

The app requests one permission:

- **Network**, limited to `https://readwise.io` and `https://api.anthropic.com`,
  for the requests described above.

It does not use the microphone, camera, location or photos.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information about
anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same place with
